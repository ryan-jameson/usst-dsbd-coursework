ABSTRACT
This paper reports on the learning outcomes of the course "Big Data Visualization
Technology" (Instructor: Ai Jun). The curriculum primarily covered the foundational
theories of data visualization, principles of visual encoding, and practical application of
mainstream visualization tools such as Excel, Gephi, and Python. Combining two
real-world scenarios—personal career planning and the "Zhicai AI" startup project—this
paper constructed nine sets of visualization charts, including career wealth evolution,
market size analysis, and capital allocation. The aim is to use data storytelling to uncover
the logic behind career development and business decision-making. The biggest difficulty
encountered during the learning process was leveraging Python to create high-dimensional
charts, specifically Sankey diagrams and nested donut charts, to intuitively illustrate the
flow and hierarchical relationships between multiple variables. To address this, I
thoroughly studied the official documentation for Matplotlib and Plotly, and successfully
achieved the precise output of aesthetically pleasing charts through repeated code
parameter debugging. This work not only validates the core value of visualization in
supporting decision-making but also demonstrates the comprehensive ability to apply
technical skills to solve real-world problems.
