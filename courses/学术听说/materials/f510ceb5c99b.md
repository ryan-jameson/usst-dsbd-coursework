## 原幻灯片 6

strategy->step 1：questionaire





In order to have a clearer understanding of user needs, we designed a survey questionnaire in the first half of the project (October) that included questions about current child nutrition problems in families, as well as inquiries about which aspects of dietary improvement were most important to families and the level of importance placed on children's nutrition by families. These surveys helped us understand whether our current work is meaningful and worthwhile, and also provided a basis for improving our future child nutrition feeding system.
The following is our questionnaire information
In the answers to the most concerning food issues for families, 93% of respondents chose the issue of hygiene of raw materials, while 77% chose the issue of whether the nutrition is balanced, which is much higher than the choice of delicious meals, indicating that the current families are paying increasing attention to nutrition and hygiene issues.
In the survey on whether families have a specific plan for children's diet, over half of the respondents chose only a rough plan, indicating that most people have the awareness of a nutritious diet, but need professional guidance and a systematic, comprehensive, and detailed plan.

## 原幻灯片 7

strategy->step 1：questionaire





The above two questions indicate that our work should essentially help families plan their nutritional diets more finely and scientifically to meet the needs of nutritious, hygienic, and palatable meals. The following questions explore common problems in children's diets.
Based on the questionnaire, most children have issues with picky, selective eating, and excessive snacking, indicating that most children often encounter problems with malnutrition or unbalanced diets. Therefore, according to the reference literature ("Urgent call to address child malnutrition: A matter of life and future."), child nutritional imbalance often coexists with overweight and obesity, overnutrition, or delayed development.
 Nutritional imbalance can also lead to a decline in immunity, resulting in more frequent illnesses. Hence, we need to construct an artificial intelligence model to analyze children's physical data, train the model with data, and have the model generate nutritional meal plans based on the body data to improve children's dietary structure. Incorporating nutritional eating as part of daily planning, along with parental supervision and management, can solve children's dietary issues.

## 原幻灯片 12

strategy->step 3:Model Improvement





The final step is to enhance the flexibility of the model by applying machine learning algorithms. We import a large amount of children's physical data from external websites and train the model with this data. The trained model can then generate nutritional guidance and meal recommendations based on input data. Such changes ensure the professionalism of the nutritional meal plans.

We have built a machine learning model using the scikit-learn library in Python, which can predict nutritional needs based on children's physical data and generate meal recommendations. We used the random forest algorithm to predict the nutritional needs of children and generate meal recommendations based on the prediction results. Such changes can ensure the flexibility of the generated results, and to some extent, guarantee the diversity and palatability of the meal plans.

## 原幻灯片 13

strategy->step 3:Model Improvement





Regarding the choice of the Random Forest algorithm for model enhancement, we believe that it is selected due to its ability to assess feature importance. This capability allows it to make predictions about nutritional needs based on a variety of complex, nonlinear physical data, and to customize personalized nutrition recommendations according to these needs. 
The Random Forest algorithm also performs well in handling the complex relationships between data, dealing with high-dimensional data and complex interactions. It can continuously update and optimize based on an increasing amount of children's physical data and nutritional feedback. The algorithm's inherent randomness, achieved by randomly selecting subsets of features to build each tree, helps reduce the risk of model overfitting, ensuring that the model has good generalization capabilities on unseen data. This also results in better adaptability and predictive accuracy when facing new data.

## 原幻灯片 15

Weaknesses and Innovations





Innovations:
Our entire project adopts a data customization feature and designs corresponding algorithms based on our customer needs. We have tailored our overall service to be close to the users and have designed a fully user-oriented platform. We utilize a random forest data optimizer to enhance our output results, making the entire nutritional meal planning system more intelligent. Additionally, the market for children's nutritional meal systems is currently a blank slate, awaiting our development.
Weaknesses:
Due to a lack of funding and manpower, we are unable to register a domain name for our website platform and cannot support the maintenance of a backend database. Our model still lacks certain data and professionalism compared to other corporate models. Moreover, the choice of the random forest algorithm may not be the optimal solution for the entire model training process, and more research on related algorithms is needed.
