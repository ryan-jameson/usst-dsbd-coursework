## 分节 1

Reflection on My Academic Presentation
The academic presentation in this term allowed me to apply techniques from my academic listening and speaking class. I focused on understanding my audience, considering their number, identity, and knowledge level. My goal was to inform and persuade, requiring a balance between data presentation and explanation.
I aimed for a less formal tone, using shorter sentences and simpler structures to make the content more accessible.
However, I identified areas for improvement, particularly in my PowerPoint slides. This semester, I presented a design for a children's nutritional meal planning system. Before the research, we conducted surveys and wrote code to design a comprehensive and user-friendly website. The PowerPoint simply displayed the code I wrote and the results of the survey. It would have been much better if I had the opportunity to show a video of the website design. That would have been a great addition. Plus,I learned that slides should have a simple background, use clear fonts like Arial, and follow the 6-by-6 rule for bullet points. In the future, I will ensure slides are uncluttered and support my oral presentation without distraction.
I also recognized the importance of body language. I need to face the audience, stand confidently, and use purposeful movements and eye contact to engage all attendees. This will help maintain a strong connection and keep the audience focused.
Structurally, I learned the value of a clear introduction with a hook and purpose statement, a main body with signposted sections, and a concise conclusion that invites questions. I will also prepare for potential Q&A topics to ensure I can address audience queries effectively.
In summary, this experience highlighted the importance of clear visual aids like PowerPoint , and confident body language. Or My efforts at academic research will be in vain.I am committed to applying these lessons to enhance my future presentations and improve my academic speaking skills.
