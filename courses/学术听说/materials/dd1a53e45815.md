文献1
来源：
[1]杨利玲,平潇.学龄前儿童营养配餐与食谱设计[J].安阳工学院学报,2016,15(02):69-71+79.DOI:10.19329/j.cnki.1673-2928.2016.02.019.
研究问题：
The literature aims to address the issue of nutritional meal planning for preschool children, particularly how to design scientifically sound and reasonable nutritional recipes based on children's physiological and nutritional needs. The research questions include:
1.What are the physiological and nutritional characteristics of preschool children?
2.How to design nutritional recipes suitable for preschool children based on their nutritional demand characteristics and the nutrient content of different foods?
3.How to ensure that the designed recipes have sufficient and reasonably proportioned nutrients to meet the nutritional needs of preschool children?
研究方法：
研究对象： preschool children aged 3 to 6 years old, who are in a critical period of growth and development and have specific requirements for nutrition.
研究工具：
Nutritional science principles: Used to analyze the nutritional needs of preschool children.
Food composition tables: Used to calculate the nutrient content in different foods.
Nutritional meal planning methods: Including the food exchange method, used to compile a weekly nutritional menu.
Actual survey results: May involve surveys on children's current dietary status to understand their actual nutritional conditions.
数据分析：
The physiological and nutritional characteristics of preschool children include a relatively slower but steady growth rate, requiring ample minerals and vitamins, especially calcium, zinc, and vitamin A.
Designed nutritional recipes should consider children's digestive capabilities, appropriately increase meal frequency, and choose foods that are nutrient-rich, small in volume, and high in density.
By applying the theory of balanced diets and nutritional meal planning procedures, it is possible to reasonably select and combine food ingredients to ensure that the nutrients in the designed recipes are sufficient and reasonably proportioned to meet the nutritional needs of preschool children.
A weekly nutritional menu compiled using the food exchange method can provide preschool children with sufficient and reasonably proportioned nutrients to meet their nutritional needs


文献2
来源：
崔晓宇;冷歆辉;王玉明*.Analysis of Nutrition Survey and Recipe Design for Preschool Children in Jinan[J].Hans Journal of Food and Nutrition Science,2020,Vol.9(4): 327-340
研究问题：
The study aims to address the nutritional imbalances, including malnutrition, overweight, and obesity, among preschool children in Jinan city. It investigates the dietary structure, eating behaviors, and nutritional status of preschool children, and designs scientific meal plans to meet their nutritional needs.
研究方法：
研究对象： preschool children aged 3 to 6 years old from a kindergarten in Jinan city.
研究工具：
Dietary Assessment: The study uses 24-hour recall and weighing methods to record the dietary intake of the children.
Nutritional Analysis Software: Software is utilized to analyze the nutritional structure based on the recorded dietary data.
Statistical Analysis: SPSS 26.0 is employed for statistical analysis, including single-factor and multi-factor Logistic regression analyses to identify risk factors for overweight and obesity.
Anthropometric Measurements: Height and weight measurements are taken to calculate BMI values.
数据分析：
Nutritional Structure: The study finds that the dietary structure of the children varies significantly across different weight groups. Children with thinness tend to have a diet low in meat and high in vegetables, while overweight and obese children often consume more high-fat, high-calorie foods.
Eating Behaviors: Eating behaviors such as fast eating and frequent snacking are identified as risk factors contributing to overweight and obesity.
Nutritional Risk Factors: The study identifies several risk factors for overweight and obesity, including short meal times, frequent consumption of high-fat and high-calorie foods, low physical activity levels, and excessive screen time.
Recipe Design Effectiveness: After implementing the designed meal plans and dietary behavior interventions, the BMI values of the children in the thinness, overweight, and obesity groups show a trend towards normalization, indicating the effectiveness of the interventions.
