## 分节 1

HELLO TEACHER, My name is Ren Jianxin, and alongside my colleague Wang Yue, I am excited to present our research plan, which addresses a pressing issue in our society: the dual burden of malnutrition and obesity in children.
Slide 1: Problem Introduction Let's start by acknowledging the problem. In the face of improving material conditions, our children are increasingly confronted with the issue of excess nutrition. Unfortunately, this has led to a widespread existence of unreasonable nutrition structures, causing malnutrition. The societal pressure of providing a "good start" in life is growing, and with it, the costs are escalating. This has created a stable and broad market around the social pain point of "children's health." Disturbingly, from 1985 to 2014, the proportion of overweight children in China increased dramatically from 1.1 percent to 20.4 percent.
Slide 2: Problem Elaboration The issue is further exacerbated by the fact that it is common for children to lack healthy diets. Parents, in general, lack professional nutrition knowledge, and the nutrition provided in schools or kindergartens is not closely integrated with family and school efforts.
Slide 3: Situation Analysis The literature aims to address the issue of nutritional meal planning for preschool children, particularly focusing on how to design scientifically sound and reasonable nutritional recipes based on children's physiological and nutritional needs. However, current approaches often rely on empirical judgment rather than utilizing new machine learning methods to intelligently prepare meals.
Slide 4: Strategy - Step 1 and 2 Our strategy involves two main steps. First, we will conduct interviews and investigations to gain an in-depth understanding of children's nutritional diet status and campus pain points. Following this, we will design machine learning data processing algorithms and a recipe design website to comprehensively design the most scientific and nutritious recipes for children.
Slide 5: Strategy - Nutrition Program We are committed to programming nutrition programs for children. This will be a classification standard based on height, weight, age, and demand, ensuring that every child receives a personalized nutrition plan.
Slide 6: Scientific Diet, Healthy Life In conclusion, we look forward to the results of our research, which we believe will contribute to a scientific diet and a healthy life for children. We are eager to embark on this journey and are confident that our work will make a significant impact.
Thank you for your attention, and we look forward to any questions you may have.
