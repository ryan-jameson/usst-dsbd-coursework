## 原幻灯片 2

Introduction
In response to game addiction, China has introduced a series of policies, in September 2021, the latest anti-addiction policy for minors was implemented, this policy is known as the strictest game control policy in history, and in 2024, at the NPC&CPPCC Annual Sessions, some representatives put forward a proposal on college students should be addicted to games.as college students, we are interested in this, and want to further study the necessity of college students' game addiction.

## 原幻灯片 3

Problem
Why is it possible for college students to become addicted to games?
If you play games to prevent addiction, how will college students react and feel about it? 
Is it necessary for college students to go to the game to prevent addiction?

## 原幻灯片 4

Method
1.Questionnaires

2. Subject-to-person interview

3. Literature combined

## 原幻灯片 5

Possible data
College students relax after three years of hard study in high school
the game itself is addictive or can bring happiness and fill the loneliness in students' hearts
most college students refuse to play games and         are resistant to it

## 原幻灯片 6

Conclusion
The core reason why college students will be addicted to games is not the lack of external compulsory control but because of their own lack of self-control management, at the same time, appropriate games can also allow college students to relax academic pressure and enjoy the happiness brought by games, in addition, college students are different from underage students, they have their own personality and thinking, mandatory policy control, may be counterproductive, so our group believes that it is not necessary for the state to prevent college students from addiction to games.

## 原幻灯片 7

Conclusion
On the other hand, some delegates proposed to focus on higher education for college students to prevent game addiction, so for the core reason, they should pay more attention to the guidance and cultivation of students' self-management in higher education.
