## 分节 1

Based on the anti-addiction policy for minors, the exploration of whether college students need to be anti-addicted 

Result（based on the questionnaire）
Figure 1

Figure 1 shows college students' perceptions of online games and their willingness to play them. The result show nearly 50% students have a neutral view of online games, while a significant percentage believe that the advantages outweigh the disadvantages. This suggests that the anti-addiction policy might face some dissatisfaction among students who see gaming as beneficial or students who have a neutral view of online games. On the other hand , From the perspective of data, online games do have certain benefits for college students, and further analysis shows that the core cause of game addiction is not the game itself, and what is this core reason, we have also presented it in the subsequent results
Figure 2

Figure 2 identifies several main factors contributing to minors' addiction to games, with "lack of self-control," "strong game attraction," and "ability to relax" being the most cited reasons. The nearly 50% response for "lack of parental and friend companionship" also suggests that family dynamics play a role in gaming addiction. This implies that anti-addiction policies should focus on enhancing minors' self-discipline and consider external factors such as parental and peer support.

Conclusion
Main purpose：We wanted to find out why teenagers are so addicted to video games and whether we should implement anti-addiction measures for college students.
Key results：The core reason why college students will be addicted to games is not the lack of external compulsory control but because of their own lack of self-control management, at the same time, appropriate games can also allow college students to relax academic pressure and enjoy the happiness brought by games, in addition, college students are different from underage students, they have their own personality and thinking, mandatory policy control, may be counterproductive.
Comment：our group believes that it is not necessary for the state to prevent college students from addiction to games. 
Limitations：The scope of our questionnaire is small, so it should cover more college students from different schools in different regions.
Future：On the other hand, some delegates proposed to focus on higher education for college students to prevent game addiction, so for the core reason, they should pay more attention to the guidance and cultivation of students' self-management in higher education.
