## 分节 1

Based on the anti-addiction policy for minors, the exploration of whether college students need to be anti-addicted
Introduction
    With the advancement of technology and the popularization of the Internet, video games have become an indispensable part of the lives of modern college students. 
According to the WHO, gaming addiction refers to a pattern of behaviour in which the use of electronic or video games is sustained or repeated, manifested by uncontrolled gaming behaviour and gaming becoming a priority behaviour in life, regardless of adverse consequences, and continuing for an extended period of time. 
According to data published by the WHO, the proportion of adolescents who are addicted to games is nearly 30%. Due to factors such as the addiction of the game itself, plenty of spare time and the lack of self-control of some college students, some college students are gradually addicted to playing online games and delay their studies, which is undoubtedly contrary to nature, additionally, this phenomenon that college students are over-indulging in games has also sparked more and more attention and discussion. Similarly, there is also the addiction of minors to games, regarding the addiction of minors to games, the state has made a corresponding response, issued relevant policies and regulations, in the anti-addiction policy for minors, stipulating that all game companies can only provide minors with one hour of play time on Saturdays, Sundays and national holidays. this policy has been implemented for two years and has also achieved good results. On the other hand, college students, whether they should follow the anti-addiction policy for minors to objectively limit their game play time, whether this is effective, and whether there is a better solution to prevent college students from becoming addicted to games, these are all questions that need to be considered.
Author Yan XiYu has found that gaming addiction in college students can have a multifaceted impact on individuals and schools. Firstly, Long gaming time can affect the sleep, diet, and physical health of college students, leading to psychological problems such as fatigue, anxiety, and depression. Next, Over-indulging in games can lead to a lack of study time and energy, which in turn affects academic performance and graduation rates. Then, gaming addiction may cause college students to neglect communication and interaction with family, friends, and classmates, which in turn can affect interpersonal relationships. Finally, Over-reliance on games can lead to reduced communication between family members and even trigger family conflicts. Therefore, it is necessary to conduct research on game addiction prevention for college students.

Method
The survey method, which involves the use of questionnaires for data collection, is a widely used research technique in various fields, including social sciences, market research, education, and health studies. Here's an overview of its origin, application, and the pros and cons associated with it:
Origin: The roots of the survey method can be traced back to the 19th century with the rise of social science disciplines seeking to understand societal trends and behaviors. Early examples include national censuses and demographic studies. The method became more sophisticated with the advent of statistical theory and sampling techniques, which allowed researchers to make inferences about larger populations based on a sample.
Application: Today, questionnaires are used to gather opinions, attitudes, behaviors, and other information from a large number of respondents. They can be administered in various formats, including paper, telephone, online, or face-to-face interviews. Surveys are used for:
1.Gathering Quantitative Data: To measure trends and patterns that can be statistically analyzed.
2.Collecting Qualitative Data: To gain insights into respondents' perspectives and experiences.
3.Assessing Public Opinion: In politics, marketing, and social issues.
4.Evaluating Programs: To determine the effectiveness of policies, services, or interventions.
For example, author Yamini J Gurjar using a semi-structured questionnaire containing the Internet Gaming Disorder Scale-Short Form to draw a conclusion that Spending more time playing online games was significantly associated with a higher risk of IGD.
Specific research:
First, conduct a literature review to understand the existing research results and theoretical frameworks. Then, design questionnaires or interview guidance questions to understand college students' attitudes, behaviors, and influences on games and entertainment. Next, through field observations or experiments, the behavior data and responses of college students in recreational activities were collected. Finally, statistical analysis methods were used to compare the differences between different groups, verify the research hypothesis and draw conclusions.

Advantages:
Cost-Effective: Surveys can reach a large number of respondents at a relatively low cost.
Time-Efficient: They allow for the rapid collection of data from a diverse group of people.
Flexibility: Surveys can be tailored to a wide range of research questions and objectives.
Anonymity: Respondents may feel more comfortable sharing sensitive information due to the anonymous nature of many surveys.
Quantifiable Results: Surveys yield data that can be easily quantified and statistically analyzed.

Disadvantages:
Non-Response Bias: Respondents may not be representative of the population, leading to skewed results.
Social Desirability Bias: Respondents might answer questions in a way that they perceive as more socially acceptable rather than truthfully.
Measurement Error: Poorly designed questions can lead to misinterpretation of data.
Limited Depth: Surveys may not capture the complexity of certain issues or behaviors.
Data Quality: The reliance on self-reported data can be a limitation, as respondents may not always provide accurate information.
The survey method is a powerful tool for research when used appropriately. Its success depends on careful questionnaire design, rigorous sampling methods, and thoughtful analysis of the data collected.

Significance:
The significance of studying whether college students need anti-addition systems lies in understanding their dependence on entertainment content and the potential impact it may have on their studies and social life.This helps in devising better educational and support strategies to assist college students in balancing their academic and personal lives.Additionally,such research contributes to raising awareness in society about the developmental environment of young adults,promoting healthy growth and comprehensive development.
